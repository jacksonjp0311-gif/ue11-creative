FAQ

Before you buy.

Can I use them on my stream?

Yes. Each character and stream-kit purchase covers one Twitch channel and one Discord server that you own or run, monetized or not. You can print physical stickers too. Running more channels? Buy one license per channel. The full terms are in LICENSE.txt inside every pack.

What's off-limits?

Reselling or sharing the files, bundling them into another pack, claiming the characters as yours, and training AI models on them.

Are these from an anime I know?

No. Every character is an original UE11 Creative OC, not from any studio or franchise, so nobody can claim them out from under your channel.

How is the art made?

Original UE11 characters, created by James P. Jackson with AI-tailored tools. UE11 Creative designs each OC and makes the art with AI-tailored tools, then sizes, checks and packages every file for Twitch and Discord. The character packs are finished PNGs, so there's nothing for you to generate.

Do the overlays and prompt pack use the same license?

No, they have their own. Golden Hour overlays cover personal and client photo or video work. Chibi Sticker Lab covers the images you generate with it, including stickers you sell. Each has its own LICENSE.txt.

Do you track me?

We count anonymous visits to improve the site; no cookies, no personal data.

How do I get the files?

You check out on Gumroad and download the zip right away. The link also comes in your receipt email.
